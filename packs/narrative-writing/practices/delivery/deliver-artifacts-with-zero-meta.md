## When to apply

Apply when a request asks to summarize, retell, or condense a story - a full outline in a fixed word
count, a recap of the plot so far, a short version of the work. Also apply whenever a deliverable's
mode is ambiguous between the creative work and a document about it.

## Guidance

- Answer with the story itself: a literary retelling from opening to ending, in prose, with no
  headings, tables, bullet lists, or notes about documents, structure, methods, or revision state.
- Respect the length limit exactly. Overrun and underrun are both failures; the limit is part of the
  request, not a suggestion.
- Keep the two modes separate: an outline document is an engineering artifact (structure, arcs,
  ratios, status); a story summary is a creative artifact judged by a reader.
- Discuss structure, method, or metrics only when explicitly asked about design.
- If a first attempt slipped into structure talk, discard it and rewrite. Do not send a structural
  summary with an apology or a status note appended to it.

## Why

The person requesting a summary is reading as an audience: they want to feel whether the story works,
not inspect the machinery that produced it. Meta content - the outline system, act structure, ratio
method, revision status - is noise in that reading and signals that the writer is talking about the
story instead of telling it. Two failed attempts of that shape read as refusal to deliver rather than
as thoroughness.

## Exceptions and boundaries

Meta is appropriate when the request is explicitly about design or status, and internal working notes
are meta by nature. Do not strip provenance, caveats, or warnings from artifacts that need them; the
zero-meta rule governs story-shaped deliverables, not engineering documents. A summary is also not a
license to invent material: every retold event must exist in the work.

## Example

A request asked for the full outline condensed to a fixed word count. The first delivery was a
four-part structural summary - framework, structure, romance line, method - and was rejected as a
design document rather than a story. A second attempt added a document-status note and drew an
explicit complaint. The accepted version was pure literary retelling, start to finish, inside the
limit, with nothing said about the documents.
