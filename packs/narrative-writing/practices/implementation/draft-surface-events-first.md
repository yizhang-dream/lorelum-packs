## When to apply

Apply during drafting passes where the scene has a job in the design: a relationship step, a planted
clue, a thematic turn. Also apply when reviewing prose that feels useful line by line but contains no
visible accident or texture.

## Guidance

Write in ordered passes instead of one pass with everything in view:

1. Surface pass only: what the audience can see and hear - events, actions, dialogue. Draft with the
   design documents closed.
2. Function pass: confirm the design lands naturally. If a character reaching out has to be the
   relationship step, the gesture must arise from the moment, not from the outline.
3. Foreshadowing pass: plants should read like accidents - a pause, a self-misreading, moving on. No
   flag lines that announce the plant, and no early reveal of the visual detail that will later
   identify an object.
4. Budget pass: apply the deletion test (remove the beat; if all that is lost is a cute fragment, it
   fails) and check density, since a scene can hit a length target while getting thinner.

Keep criteria and design documents as verifiers for the later passes, never as prompts while writing
the surface pass.

## Why

Once a system exists to catch loose structure, the dominant failure flips: instead of invisible
structure you get visible structure, where every line does a job and the audience senses an author
rather than events. A design function only works when it is hidden under audience-visible events. The
reaction to aim for is "why did that feel strange just now", not "this scene is advancing the
relationship".

## Exceptions and boundaries

Deliberate devices that are themselves the audience-facing surface - a narrator's aside, a stylized
reveal - are not violations. The passes check design, foreshadowing, and budget; they do not replace
continuity checks or proofreading, and a scene may legitimately fail the deletion test when its job
is spectacle rather than attachment.

## Example

A scene whose design job was "she offers first" was drafted from the outline and read as engineered:
the offer arrived in a line that existed only to advance the pairing. The rewrite put the offer
inside a routine exchange as one action among several, turned the plant into a pause and a
self-misreading, and targets the outcome this method asks for: a budget check that passes, and a
reviewer who knows the outline yet cannot point to the line carrying the function.
