## When to apply

Apply when planning beats that advance a plot engine in a story sold on character or romance, and
when reviewing arcs where the puzzle line is progressing while the relationship has not moved.

## Guidance

- Hold the three-simultaneous rule for every plot beat: one answered question, one new facet of a
  character, and one change in a relationship. Missing any of the three means the two lines have
  started running in parallel instead of together.
- Track the initiative quota of the proactive lead. After a run of unprompted gestures, cap further
  proactive moves and let the other lead or an external event open the next contact.
- Accept beats with character questions, not only function questions: would the audience watch this
  character do something with no plot value at all? Add a test question that removes all plot
  information and asks whether the audience still wants to stay.
- Start or repair a relationship line with unstructured shared time - a beat that is neither
  investigation nor task - rather than adding sweetness to existing beats.
- Give the leads a genuine methodological or values conflict to play; earned disagreement is what
  puts two people visibly in the same scene.

## Why

Mystery-romance work commonly fails because the author loves the puzzle and the audience loves the
characters. A case line can advance through puzzle mechanics; a relationship cannot - it advances by
one person becoming part of the other's ordinary days. If plot beats deliver answers only, the case
matures while the cast stays flat, and the story loses the thing it was sold on.

## Exceptions and boundaries

Rare, short set pieces may carry plot function alone; the rule targets beats that are supposed to
build attachment. Stories without a romantic line use the weaker form - every beat still adds a
character facet. Do not read the quota as banning all initiative later: it shifts who opens the next
loop, not whether contact continues.

## Example

A draft's investigation beats each resolved a puzzle element, and a review found the relationship
unchanged across three arcs while the case had advanced steadily. The revision did not add romantic
banter: each case beat was re-planned to also give the character a new side and to shift the pair's
footing, and the proactive lead's gestures were capped after a run of four so an outside event had to
reopen contact. The next playtest adds a diagnostic question - would you keep watching with all
mystery information removed - designed so that a plot-only beat fails it.
